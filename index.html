COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about booking mehndi artists and services with MehndiKar.

How can I book a mehndi artist in Bengaluru?

You can book through the MehndiKar website by selecting your preferred mehndi service and entering your contact details, location, date and preferred timing. After submitting your request, our team will consider the number of people, design type, artist availability and service duration to suggest a suitable schedule. Our team will contact you to discuss your requirements.

Do you provide doorstep mehndi services?

Yes. You can request mehndi services at your home or preferred location in Bengaluru, subject to artist availability, preferred timing and service location.

Can I book mehndi for weddings and special occasions?

Yes. MehndiKar offers booking options for bridal functions, engagements, baby showers, festivals, family celebrations, corporate events and other special occasions.

How much does mehndi service cost?

Our listed mehndi services start from ₹1,499 for 2 hours. For hourly packages, the final charges depend on the selected service duration. Customized premium bridal designs are available on enquiry.

Can I request customized bridal mehndi designs?

Yes. You can request customized bridal designs, including detailed patterns and personalized elements. Submit your requirements through the booking form to discuss the design and pricing.

Do you offer mehndi services for multiple guests?

Yes. You can submit a booking request for multiple people. Please mention the number of people and event details so that our team can discuss the required number of artists and suitable timing.

Can I book a mehndi artist for a specific date and time?

Yes. You can provide your preferred date and session timing in the booking form. Confirmation is subject to artist availability.

Do you sell fresh mehndi cones and aftercare products?

Yes. MehndiKar offers fresh mehndi cones, nail cones, aftercare balm, aftercare kits, sealing tape and gloves. You can select a product through the booking form to enquire.

Is advance payment required to confirm a booking?

Yes. An advance payment is necessary to confirm your booking. Our team will share the advance payment details and confirmation process after discussing your requirements.

How will I receive confirmation after submitting a booking request?

After submitting your request, our team will contact you using the phone number provided to discuss availability, requirements and booking confirmation.
